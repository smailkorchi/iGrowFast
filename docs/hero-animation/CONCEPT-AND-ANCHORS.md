# Hero animation — winning concept (The Escapement, grafted) — 2026-08-31

Owner rejected the old look wholesale. 4-concept panel + 3 judges (workflow wf_faeefc44-864).
Winner: The Escapement (2/3 judges) with mandatory grafts: (1) Living Signal flow physics replace watchmaker precision (diffusion smears gear teeth); (2) Moroccan khatem eight-pointed star hub + eight-lobed iris rings (from Zellige Engine); (3) centre-weighted re-crop for mobile.

## Palette
- `#070C16` — Field / deep ground — the dark plate the whole mechanism sits on. Replaces the rejected cream world entirely.
- `#101A2B` — Chassis fill — solid bodies of the wheel, gate plates, collector column.
- `#22314A` — Structure lines — rails, tines, bearing marks, non-active geometry. 1px-equivalent uniform stroke.
- `#5B7CFA` — Cobalt signal — CARRIER family lanes and their tokens (comb A).
- `#9A7CF7` — Violet signal — MESSAGING family lanes (WhatsApp instances, Email, Web Push). Deliberately not green, never a bubble.
- `#34D3B0` — Mint-teal signal — DATA family (Google Sheets ingestion, R2 storage vault, verification/GeoIP service, Data Imports).
- `#FFB454` — Amber signal — ADS/ANALYTICS family (Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Campaign Analytics) and gate-flick highlights.
- `#E8B54A` — Gold under-rail — the reconciliation/ledger line running beneath the lower gate rail. Money as an accounting flow line only; no coins, no notes, no wallet.
- `#EAF1FF` — Ice-white — the reconciled output tokens and the single exit stream; the brightest thing in frame, and the only thing that touches the left third.

## World
A flat-vector precision instrument seen dead-on, as if the back plate of a mechanical watch had been redrawn as an operations diagram: thin uniform strokes, flat fills, crisp corners, no texture, no paper, no shading beyond a single soft gradient inside the escapement wheel. The ground is deep midnight-navy, not black — a cold engineered dark that makes the coloured signal lanes read as luminous without glowing like neon. Everything is orthogonal or radial; every moving element is the same rounded-square token, so difference is carried by colour and lane, never by shape novelty. The mechanism is weighted into the right two-thirds of the frame; the left third is intentionally near-empty dark plate with only one thin ice-white output rail crossing it low, so a large headline can sit in clean negative space. The overall read is watchmaker, not factory: small, exact, quiet, expensive.

## Timeline
- **0.00s** STATE A (the loop anchor). Tokens sit evenly staggered on all eighteen intake rails; the escapement wheel's tooth #1 is exactly on the pawl; both gate rails carry tokens on exact pitch; the merged output stream is continuous and evenly spaced; the specular light sweep is at the far right edge.
- **0.0–2.5s** Intake descent. Cobalt tokens run down the nine carrier tines top-right (three tines solid and bright, six dimmed and dashed, carrying fainter ghost tokens). Violet tokens run down the messaging tines upper-middle; teal tokens down the data tines; amber tokens slide inward along the four right-edge horizontal tines. All at slightly different phase, none in unison — the frame reads busy but ordered.
- **2.0–4.0s** The merge. Every tine turns ninety degrees into the vertical collector column at the right. Tokens queue into strict single file, colours interleaving, spacing normalising — the first visible act of order.
- **3.0–6.0s** The escapement. The single file feeds the large toothed wheel at upper right. The wheel advances tooth-by-tooth, not smoothly: a crisp micro-tick every 0.4s, pawl lifting and dropping. Each tick releases exactly one token. This is the emotional centre — the moment the chaos becomes a rate.
- **3.5–7.5s** The gate banks. A splitter under the wheel alternates released tokens onto the upper gate rail (nine gate plates) and the lower gate rail (eight plates), both running right-to-left. Each plate flicks open for exactly one token then snaps shut — producing a rolling ripple of gate-flicks travelling right to left across both rails, amber on the leading edge of each flick.
- **5.5–8.0s** The ledger line. As tokens clear the money-side gates on the lower rail, a thin gold under-rail illuminates progressively beneath them, right to left — a running reconciliation line, purely a line, never a coin, note, stack or wallet.
- **7.0–9.2s** The confluence. The two gate rails converge at a junction left of the mechanism; tokens interleave into ONE stream of identical ice-white tokens on perfect pitch. The stream passes through five small iris rings that open and close in sequence, each iris briefly revealing a different subset of the token's inner facets — the same stream, five permissions.
- **9.2–10.00s** Return to STATE A. The stream exits the left edge; the specular sweep completes exactly one right-to-left traversal and wraps to the right edge; wheel tooth #1 lands back on the pawl; every token on every rail occupies the exact position it held at 0.00s. The final frame is pixel-equivalent to the first.

## Camera
Locked off. Static frame, flat head-on orthographic view with a wide flat field of view; the entire mechanism lies parallel to the picture plane. No push-in, no pull-back, no pan, no tilt, no drift, no zoom, no rack focus, no handheld shake, no parallax. All motion is internal to the mechanism — this is what makes the loop mathematically exact and what keeps the frame usable as a background under type.

## Loop mechanic
The whole clip is one cycle of a periodic mechanism, tuned so frame 0 and frame 10.00s are geometrically identical. (1) The escapement wheel has 24 teeth and advances exactly 24 tooth-steps — one full 360° revolution — in 10s, one tick per 0.4167s, returning tooth #1 to the pawl. (2) Intake tokens are spaced 0.10 frame-height apart and travel exactly 0.50 frame-height in 10s = five whole spacings, so each rail looks unchanged. (3) Gate-rail tokens sit on a pitch equal to the gate spacing (0.06 frame-width) and travel exactly 0.60 frame-width in 10s = ten whole pitches. (4) The 17 gate plates run a ripple whose period divides 10s evenly, so every plate is closed at 0s and at 10s. (5) The five irises complete exactly two open-close cycles each. (6) The specular light sweep makes exactly one right-to-left traversal and wraps at the edge. Nothing fades in, nothing fades out, no element is introduced mid-clip that is absent at 0s — the camera never moves, so there is no camera state to reconcile. The prompt states the loop requirement explicitly; delivery still gets a 0.25s matched crossfade at the seam as insurance against sub-pixel drift from the generator.

## Risks
1) COUNT FIDELITY IS THE BIG ONE. Seedance will not reliably render exactly nine tines, exactly nine plus eight gate plates, or exactly five irises. Do not let the DOM chips dock to detected video features. Dock them to the fixed normalized coordinate map above, and — recommended — draw the anchor furniture (tick marks, the small connector nubs, the iris outlines) as a crisp inline-SVG layer between the video and the chips. The video then supplies the world, motion and light; the SVG supplies the exact geometry the chips attach to. This also fixes the solid-vs-dashed carrier distinction, which the model will almost certainly flatten.

2) LOOP DRIFT. Generators rarely return to the exact first frame even when told to. Budget for a 0.25s matched crossfade at the seam, and pick the take whose 10.0s frame is closest to 0.0s. Do NOT ping-pong reverse — the flow is directional and reversing it reads as the machine running backwards, which inverts the whole message.

3) CAMERA CREEP. "Locked off" is frequently ignored; a slow push-in will sneak in and it breaks both the loop and the headline's calm zone. Reject any take with visible drift rather than trying to stabilise it — stabilisation crops, and the crop eats the left third.

4) TEXT LEAKAGE. Even with suppression, expect glyph-like artefacts on gate plates and the wheel hub. Inspect at 200%. Any faux-lettering is an automatic reject: legal posture here is that the video carries zero marks, and a smeared pseudo-logo is worse than none.

5) LEFT-THIRD ENCROACHMENT. The model likes to fill empty space. If it does, the headline dies. Mitigation if a take is otherwise perfect: a left-edge scrim gradient from #070C16 at 100% to 0% across x 0–0.40, which is consistent with the palette and costs nothing.

6) COLOUR COMPLIANCE. Watch that the violet messaging lane does not drift green in grade — any green-on-dark bubble-adjacent read near the messaging tines is a WhatsApp-cliché hazard. And confirm the gold under-rail stays a LINE; if the model renders it as a pooling glow it starts to read as a pile of money, which is the compliance line under Art 182.

7) DENSITY vs BACKGROUND DUTY. Eighteen intake rails is a lot of motion for something sitting behind a headline. If it fights the type in review, the fix is to drop intake token opacity to ~55% and keep the output stream at full brightness — the hierarchy (chaos dim, order bright) is the argument anyway.

8) SCRUB ENCODE. Per the existing homepage motion rules this clip must survive `currentTime` scrubbing: deliver with -g 8 desktop / -g 4 mobile and extract a poster frame at 7.6s (both rails full, stream merged, irises visible) — that frame alone has to sell the concept for reduced-motion and no-JS visitors.
